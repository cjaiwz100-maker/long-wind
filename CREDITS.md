- Chinese jian (straight sword) motion `src/character/mocap/jian-demo.js`: pose keys tracked from a reference video

  video frames are included).

  lighting model, grass wind, camera rig) were studied and re-implemented from scratch in this codebase; a few small
  standard formulas (e.g. the ACES fit, lamp falloff) match it. No assets from it are used.
- Everything else (terrain, grass, bamboo, the town, sky, weather, townsfolk, procedural animation clips, all music
  and sound effects, voices) is generated procedurally at load — there are no audio files in this repository.
